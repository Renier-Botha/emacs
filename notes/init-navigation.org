#+TITLE: init-navigation.el — Consult and Avy
#+PROPERTY: header-args :tangle no

* Purpose

Configures enhanced search/navigation commands: ~consult~ for
buffer/line/grep/xref navigation, and ~avy~ for jumping directly to a
visible character/word/line on screen.

* Walkthrough

** Consult

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(use-package consult
  :bind (("C-x b"   . consult-buffer)
         ("C-x 4 b" . consult-buffer-other-window)
         ("C-x p b" . consult-project-buffer)
         ("M-y"     . consult-yank-pop)
         ("M-g g"   . consult-goto-line)
         ("M-g M-g" . consult-goto-line)
         ("M-g i"   . consult-imenu)
         ("M-g o"   . consult-outline)
         ("M-s l"   . consult-line)
         ("M-s g"   . consult-ripgrep)
         ("M-s f"   . consult-find)
         :map minibuffer-local-map
         ("M-s" . consult-history)
         ("M-r" . consult-history))
  :custom
  (consult-preview-key 'any)
  (register-preview-function #'consult-register-format)
  :init
  (setq xref-show-xrefs-function #'consult-xref
        xref-show-definitions-function #'consult-xref))
#+end_src

[[https://github.com/minad/consult][Consult]] replaces or augments a
number of built-in commands with enhanced, often live-previewed,
equivalents:
- ~C-x b~ / ~C-x 4 b~ / ~C-x p b~: switch buffer (current window,
  other window, project-scoped), replacing the default
  ~switch-to-buffer~ family with an enhanced, annotated, previewable
  version.
- ~M-y~: browse and pick from kill-ring history (~consult-yank-pop~)
  instead of just cycling blindly with repeated ~M-y~.
- ~M-g g~ / ~M-g M-g~: go to line by number.
- ~M-g i~: jump to a definition via Imenu, with live preview.
- ~M-g o~: jump to a heading via Outline.
- ~M-s l~: fuzzy-search visible lines in the current buffer.
- ~M-s g~: ripgrep search across the project/directory, with live
  preview of matches.
- ~M-s f~: find files by name.
- Inside the minibuffer itself (~minibuffer-local-map~), ~M-s~/~M-r~
  are bound to browse minibuffer history interactively.

Custom settings:
- ~consult-preview-key~ set to ~'any~: preview the candidate under
  point on *any* keypress/movement while browsing (rather than
  requiring an explicit preview key), so results are always visible
  live -- but see the note below on expensive commands.
- ~register-preview-function~ set to Consult's formatter, so register
  previews (e.g. ~C-x r j~) look consistent with the rest of the
  Consult UI.
- In ~:init~ (i.e. before Consult itself is necessarily loaded),
  ~xref-show-xrefs-function~ and ~xref-show-definitions-function~ are
  redirected to ~consult-xref~, so that ~xref-find-definitions~/
  ~xref-find-references~ (used by Eglot, see ~init-eglot.el~ and the
  ~C-c c d~/~C-c c r~ leader bindings) show their results through
  Consult's UI instead of the default xref buffer.

** Avy

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(use-package avy
  :bind (("C-'"   . avy-goto-char-timer)
         ("M-g w" . avy-goto-word-1)
         ("M-g W" . avy-goto-word-2)
         ("M-g l" . avy-goto-line))
  :custom
  (avy-timeout-seconds 0.3)
  (avy-style 'at-full))
#+end_src

[[https://github.com/abo-abo/avy][Avy]] lets you jump directly to any
visible character/word/line by typing a short sequence and then
selecting from overlayed hint labels -- much faster than repeated
cursor movement for on-screen jumps.
- ~C-'~: jump to a character, typed with a timeout (so you can type
  more than one character to narrow down matches before avy shows
  labels).
- ~M-g w~ / ~M-g W~: jump to a word, matching on its first
  1 or 2 characters respectively.
- ~M-g l~: jump to the start of a visible line.
- ~avy-timeout-seconds~: how long avy waits for more input before
  treating what's typed so far as final and showing the hint labels.
- ~avy-style~ set to ~'at-full~: hint labels are overlayed directly
  on/replacing the matched text (rather than e.g. before it),
  covering the full match.

These same commands are also reachable through the leader key (~C-c
j ...~), see ~init-keybindings.el~.
